# Why I am receiving not authorized messages?
{: #troubleshoot-not-authorized-1}
{: troubleshoot}
{: support}

I am receiving not authorized messages on my VPC.
{: shortdesc}

You are receiving a 401 or 403 errors indicating you are not authorized.
{: tsSymptoms}

Your account might not be authorized for VPC.
{: tsCauses}

Make sure that you are using an account that has been onboarded.
{: tsResolve}

For more information, see [Managing IAM access for VPC Infrastructure Services](/docs/vpc?topic=vpc-iam-getting-started&interface=cli) and [Authentication](/docs/apis/vpc/latest#api-authentication) in the VPC API content.
